MINIMO · Android

Remind v1.1

A clean, distraction-free event reminders app for Android with a home screen widget that keeps your next important date in sight without cluttering your day.

Google Play ↗ All MINIMO apps

Features

Home Screen Widget

See your next upcoming event and countdown directly on your home screen, with one-tap shortcuts to view the full list or create a new reminder.

Flexible Reminders

Configure multiple custom notifications per event — minutes, hours, days or weeks in advance — scheduled reliably across device reboots.

Tags & Calendar View

Organize events with color-coded tags, instant search, date and tag filters, a monthly calendar view and an archive for past events.

Offline-First & Local Backup

Your data stays on your device in a local Room database, with full JSON backup export and restore whenever you switch phones.

Architecture & Stack

Built natively in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, Room for offline persistence, Android AlarmManager and BootReceiver for exact reminder scheduling, and Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics for reliability.
